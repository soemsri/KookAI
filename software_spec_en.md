# KookAI Mobile App - Software Specification

> 💡 **In Short:**  
> **KookAI** is a mobile app that lets you chat with and control a smart AI assistant running on your computer from your phone. It is like having your computer's AI helper right inside your pocket!

---

## 🌟 What Can This App Do? (Main Features)

### 1. 📱 Easy Computer Connection (Pairing)
- **Scan QR Code or Type 6 Digits:** Simply open the app and scan the QR code on your computer screen, or type in a 6-digit PIN number to connect instantly.
- **Auto Remember:** The app remembers your computer after connecting once, so you don't need to type the PIN every time.

### 2. 💬 Chat with AI Like a Friend (AI Chat Companion)
- **Ask Anything:** Type questions, ask for help with homework, or tell the AI to write computer code for you.
- **Choose AI Brains:** Easily switch between different AI models (like Gemini, Claude, Codex, Kimi, or Grok).

### 3. 🗣️ Talk Using Your Voice (Voice Input)
- **Speak Instead of Typing:** Just tap the microphone button and speak! The app turns your voice into text automatically—great when you don't feel like typing.

### 4. 📷 Send Photos and Files (Image & Document Sharing)
- **Take Photos or Pick Pictures:** Snap a picture using your camera or choose a photo from your gallery, then ask the AI questions about it.
- **Attach Documents:** Send files to the AI so it can read and summarize them for you.

### 5. 💻 Switch Between Multiple Computers (Server Switcher)
- **Manage Multiple Servers:** If you have more than one computer (for example, at home and at work), you can save them all in the app and switch between them anytime.

### 6. ⚡ Live Answers in Real Time (Live Streaming)
- **Watch AI Type:** Answers appear word by word as the AI thinks, so you don't have to wait around.
- **Clean & Easy to Read:** Text and computer code are neatly organized with clear boxes and colors that are easy on the eyes.

### 7. 📋 Copy Text and Code in One Tap (One-Tap Copy)
- **Quick Copy Button:** Tap the copy button on any answer or code block to copy it to your clipboard instantly.

---

## 🛠️ What You Need to Use KookAI
1. **Android Phone:** An Android smartphone with the KookAI app installed.
2. **Computer:** A computer running the KookAI Server software.
3. **Internet / Wi-Fi:** An active network connection to link your phone to your computer.
